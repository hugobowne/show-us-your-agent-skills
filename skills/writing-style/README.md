# writing-style

A personal writing-style skill that pushes agent prose toward Zinsser-first plainness, deletion, and critique before the human adds taste.

## who showed it

Isaac Flath is an AI and product engineer who builds local tools for writing, teaching, private knowledge, and real workflows. In Episode 5 he described `writing-style` as one of the skills that powers his Raw2Draft writing loop.

## what it does

The skill gives the agent a writing lens before it drafts, edits, critiques, or revises prose. Isaac frames writing skills as personal: they encode what the writer wants cut, what counts as bloat, which editor or author lens to invoke, and where the agent should stop before pretending to have taste.

> *"Depending on what I want to do or what I feel like the problem is, I kind of invoke a particular, you know, author or editor."* [\[01:28:36\]](https://youtube.com/live/6zju7hyCFl0?t=5316)

In Isaac's workflow, `writing-style` composes with critique and long-form source material. He often begins with a long transcript of himself talking, asks the agent to organize and simplify it, then uses critique to decide what should change next.

> *"Writing style skill, no surprise there. and all of these kind of compose together. So if I ask it to write something, it'll typically grab the writing style, it'll write it. It'll do a critique, it'll take that critique, tell me what to do different."* [\[01:30:51\]](https://youtube.com/live/6zju7hyCFl0?t=5451)

## why it's notable

The skill is notable because it does not try to make the agent sound finished. Isaac's taste move is the opposite: make the model cut, simplify, and get out of the way.

> *"Generally you can take something that AI wrote and cut like fifty percent of it and it'll still say the same thing, just clearer."* [\[01:29:39\]](https://youtube.com/live/6zju7hyCFl0?t=5379)

He treats style as partly universal and partly personal. Universal rules catch empty openings and vague filler. Personal taste comes later, after the model has made the draft plain enough for him to take over.

> *"I'm trying to get it to cut as much out as possible and be as plain as possible, even if it's like whatever, because I'm gonna add the taste in."* [\[01:52:02\]](https://youtube.com/live/6zju7hyCFl0?t=6722)

That makes this a good repo stub rather than a reconstructed skill: we know the philosophy and trigger, but not Isaac's authoritative instruction file.
